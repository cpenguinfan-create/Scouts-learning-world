Information for parents and guardians

Scout's Learning World is a free early-learning experience. There are no child accounts, purchases, advertising, or public messaging features in this version. Stars and activity preferences are stored in this browser on this device, not in an online child profile. You can clear them by clearing this site's browser storage.

Privacy

We aim to minimize personal information. The site itself does not ask children to provide their names, contact details or locations. Before public launch, the hosting provider's logs, analytics, cookies, third-party services, and actual data handling must be reviewed and a complete privacy notice published.

Contact us

Parents and guardians: contact details will be added after the domain and dedicated contact address are configured. Children should ask a grown-up for help and should not submit personal information.

Terms and accessibility

Activities and rewards are free and virtual. A final review of Canadian privacy, consumer protection, accessibility, intellectual property, and language obligations is required before public launch.
